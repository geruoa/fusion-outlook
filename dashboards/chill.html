Data and method Regional series were read from Fig. 2 (±0.2 CP), Fig. 3 and Supplementary Fig. 6 of Jha, A, Pathak & Velicogna (2026) and checked against the paper's reported means, standard deviations, CMIP6 variability ratio and projection counts. Supplementary Table 1 is transcribed exactly. Counts at the median threshold reported in the paper come from the gridded data and can differ from the plotted series by a year where values sit very close to the threshold. The mean-shift comparison is an illustration computed in your browser. Year labels follow the paper's figures. Processed data are on Zenodo (10.5281/zenodo.20058559).
