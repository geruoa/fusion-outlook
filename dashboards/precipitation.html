Which climate modes explain wet months

Share of grid cells whose best model selected each predictor. Interactions between modes dominate; no single index appears in more than 6% of cells.

Interaction of two modesSingle mode

Over 90% of grid-cell models keep three predictors or fewer. Explanatory power is higher in coastal and inland areas (R² ≈ 0.28 in the San Francisco Bay Area) and lowest in the Sierra Nevada, where most of the state's water originates.

Data and method The statewide series is the latitude-weighted PRISM mean, read from Fig. 3b of Shalgumbayeva, A & Velicogna (2026) to about ±5 mm and checked by reproducing the figure's 5-year running mean. Window statistics, ranks and classes are computed from that series in your browser; percentiles are empirical within each window, and the 20th/80th percentile classes are for illustration. Regional changes are from Fig. 2e (±3 mm) and predictor frequencies from Fig. 5d (±0.1 percentage points). Grid-cell analyses (area fractions, R²) are as reported in the paper.
