Dashboards

Three dashboards turn the published results into tools you can explore. Each one lists its data sources and how the numbers were obtained.

Study 2 · Predict

MJO forecast skill

Compare correction methods by lead time and MJO strength, test whether history helps, check seasonality, and browse 2,080 FuXi-S2S ensemble forecasts (2002–2021) in phase space.

Open

Study 1 · Diagnose

Precipitation baselines

See how a water year ranks against a 30-year normal versus a recent window, follow moving 20-year statistics, and compare regional and seasonal losses and the climate modes selected.

Open

Study 3 · Impact

Orchard chill risk

Set a crop threshold and count shortfall years in observations and projections, separate the effect of variability from the mean trend, and check forecast accuracy by lead.

About the numbersPublished curves and series were read from the articles' figures at full resolution and checked against values stated in the text. Exact tables are transcribed as published. Forecast trajectories in the MJO browser come directly from the public FuXi-S2S RMM archive released with Study 2. Where the original data files become available, they replace the digitized values without changes to the dashboards.
